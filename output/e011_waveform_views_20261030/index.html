What the two response views and statistics mean

Waveform-specific deviation is the context/time/position-adjusted waveform effect from E011, with the common waveform response removed. It asks whether a distinguishing waveform deviation repeats.

Total observed response retains the entire response for that waveform, giving its observed comparison cells equal weight. A repeatable total response can be shared by every waveform, or reflect repeatable artifacts/context. It is not evidence of waveform identity information.

At a single electrode × bin, each half supplies one number: a correlation is undefined. The heatmap instead shows that waveform's mean A×B product across 100 splits, signed full-data response, RMS half-to-half disagreement or fraction of splits with matching signs.

Temporal pattern r compares that waveform's A/B time courses at one electrode over fixed [50,1050) ms, then takes the median across splits. Joint pattern r compares the flattened 14-electrode × one-second patterns. These do not correlate across the five waveform identities or across complementary split repetitions. Coarse 200-ms temporal correlations contain only five time bins; all sampled time points are correlated after filtering.

Paired splits, trial eligibility, 25-ms derivative estimator and all six bin widths exactly match the original E011. Pointwise products retain squared physical units. Large products can reflect large unstable responses; read them alongside disagreement and pattern correlations. The optional group table resamples participants, not splits. No binary repeatable/not-repeatable threshold, corrected significance test or feature selection is supplied.

Limits and downloadable outputs

Ten participants; 595 pairs / 1,190 events, identical to E011. Sparse waveform/position support can make estimates unstable. Split halves share comparison contexts; total responses also retain unadjusted nuisance activity. A repeatable response is not necessarily neural, waveform-distinct or decodable on held-out comparisons. RMS derivatives may reflect noise. 1-ms sample spacing does not imply independent 1-ms physiological resolution. All findings are exploratory.
